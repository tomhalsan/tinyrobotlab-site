Why This Exists

Hands-On Engineering Over Abstract Descriptions

Modern autonomous agents are often described with commercial hype or buried inside monolithic frameworks. Tiny Robot Lab was created to deconstruct these concepts by implementing them from scratch: asynchronous messaging queues, dynamic capability registration, heartbeat liveness policies, evidence-conditioned synthesis, and provider-neutral Clean Architecture boundaries.
